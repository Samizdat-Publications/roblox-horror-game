# Critic consult: strange-boats (gemini-4-neon)

Date: 11/27/2026. Files shown: 35-boats-before-near.jpg, 36-boats-before-far.jpg, 37-boats-after-near.jpg, 38-boats-after-far.jpg. Finish: STOP.

## Question

These four stills are from a co-op Roblox horror fishing game (ages 10 and up, often on phones). On the last night, strange boats appear on the water during the run home. The rule: there are no other boats on this lake, never wave back, never row toward them. Images 1 and 2 are BEFORE (one boat at about 50 studs, then a boat at about 85 studs beside a false beacon on a pole). Images 3 and 4 are AFTER the same two views: the strange boat's lantern now hangs behind a tall standing figure inside a soft glow, with pin eyes and a streak of light on the water.

Score 0 to 10, BEFORE and AFTER, for each:
1. reads_as_boat: at a glance on a phone, is it clearly a boat with someone in it?
2. distinct_from_beacon: can you tell it apart from the false beacon light on a pole (image 2 and 4)?
3. creepy: does it feel wrong and threatening, not silly?
4. art_fit: does the glow look like part of the world or like a UI sticker?
Then list the three most useful changes, blunt, under 350 words, no em dashes.

## Answer

**Scores (0 to 10)**

1. **reads_as_boat:** BEFORE 2 | AFTER 5
   Before, it reads as a pole on a crate, especially at 85 studs. After, the standing figure reads clearly on a phone, but the boat faces dead-on and still looks like a tiny flat box or pedestal.
2. **distinct_from_beacon:** BEFORE 2 | AFTER 8
   Before, both are just glowing dots on vertical sticks. After, the backlit silhouette makes it unmistakable from the pole beacon even at 85 studs.
3. **creepy:** BEFORE 3 | AFTER 3
   Before is too muddy to scare anyone. After fixes visibility, but it looks goofy instead of threatening. The eyes are way too huge, bulging off the small square head like cartoon frog eyes, and the stiff blocky body looks like a friendly stickman.
4. **art_fit:** BEFORE 7 | AFTER 2
   Before fits the quiet, dark Roblox aesthetic. After looks pasted on. The glow breaks into five hard concentric rings like a bullseye target, and the water streak looks like three floating green rectangles.

**Three Most Useful Changes**

1. **Turn the boat at a 45 to 70 degree angle.** A rowboat viewed head-on is just a brown rectangle on a phone screen. Turning it sideways instantly exposes the curved hull, bow, and oars so it reads as a boat at a glance without needing heavy lighting tricks.
2. **Shrink the eyes and fix the figure silhouette.** The eyes need to be tiny pinpricks set deep inside a slightly wider head, or hidden until the player gets closer. Give the figure a long coat, slumped shoulders, or a wide brimmed hat (like a dark fisherman) so it reads as an eerie human shape instead of a toy stick figure.
3. **Replace the banded glow and boxy water streak.** Swap the stacked ring effect for one soft, low-opacity radial gradient billboard that fades smoothly to zero, tinted warm like the other lanterns. Delete the segmented rectangles on the water and either use a single blurry, soft-edged reflection decal or let a low PointLight cast a natural pool of light on the surface.

## Action taken

Round 1 scored reads_as_boat 5, distinct 8, creepy 3, art_fit 2. Taken: the hull turned side-on, a long coat and brimmed hat, smaller eyes, a smoother glow, the water streak removed.
