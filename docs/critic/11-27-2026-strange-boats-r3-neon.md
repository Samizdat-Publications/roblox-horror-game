# Critic consult: strange-boats-r3 (gemini-4-neon)

Date: 11/27/2026. Files shown: 35-boats-before-near.jpg, 36-boats-before-far.jpg, 37-boats-after-near.jpg, 38-boats-after-far.jpg, 39-boats-fisherman-close.jpg. Finish: STOP.

## Question

These four stills are from a co-op Roblox horror fishing game (ages 10 and up, often on phones). On the last night, strange boats appear on the water during the run home. The rule: there are no other boats on this lake, never wave back, never row toward them. Images 1 and 2 are BEFORE (one boat at about 50 studs, then a boat at about 85 studs beside a false beacon on a pole). Images 3 and 4 are AFTER the same two views, and image 5 is a close crop of image 3: the strange boat now lies turned side-on, a tall dark figure in a long coat and a wide brimmed hat stands in it with pinprick eyes, and its lantern hangs from a hooked pole beside his shoulder inside a soft glow, and the boat has a pointed bow and taller sides.

Score 0 to 10, BEFORE and AFTER, for each:
1. reads_as_boat: at a glance on a phone, is it clearly a boat with someone in it?
2. distinct_from_beacon: can you tell it apart from the false beacon light on a pole (image 2 and 4)?
3. creepy: does it feel wrong and threatening, not silly?
4. art_fit: does the glow look like part of the world or like a UI sticker?
Then list the three most useful changes, blunt, under 350 words, no em dashes.

## Answer

**Scores (BEFORE / AFTER)**

1. **reads_as_boat:** 2 / 6
Before, it looked like a floating crate with a post. After, it clearly shows a figure on something floating, but the hull reads as a flat rectangular raft or plank rather than a boat.
2. **distinct_from_beacon:** 2 / 8
In image 2, it blends right into the beacon on a phone screen. In image 4, the wide base, tall silhouette, and glow make it immediately distinct.
3. **creepy:** 3 / 6
Before was too unclear to feel threatening. After hits a solid "hat man" vibe for ages 10+, but the giant centered halo and stiff blocky shape drift slightly silly up close.
4. **art_fit:** 7 / 3
Before matched the game's lighting cleanly. After has a huge, visibly banded 2D circle that looks like a pasted UI sticker, clashing with the crisp, fog-free light of the beacon and the player's own lantern.

**Three Most Useful Changes**

1. **Fix the sticker glow.** Right now the giant halo is centered behind the man's torso like a saint's halo instead of coming from the lantern. Center it strictly on the lantern, cut its radius in half, lower its opacity to hide the color banding, and put a smaller matching haze on beacons and the player lantern so it reads as lake mist rather than a UI decal.
2. **Make the hull silhouette unmistakably a boat.** Flat side-on, your pointed bow disappears and the boat reads as a brown brick. Curve the sheer line so the bow and stern tips rise clearly higher than the middle, angle the boat slightly (a three-quarter view instead of dead 90 degrees), and add an oar resting visibly out the side.
3. **Break the rigid rectangle silhouette.** A perfectly straight black block with bright dots for eyes edges into cartoon territory. Flare the bottom of the coat slightly, tilt the head or droop the hat brim a few degrees, and dim the eyes slightly so he feels watching and wrong instead of goofy.

## Action taken

Round 3 scored 6, 8, 6, 3. Taken: the glow halved and warmed, raised bow and stern tips, a flared coat hem, the head and hat tilted.
