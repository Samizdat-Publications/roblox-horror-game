Clip 1, in order, roughly every 6 to 7 seconds from the start: (1) the Neighbor: a rusty oarlock creaking slowly far across the water, with nobody rowing; (2) three soft knocks on the underside of a wooden boat; (3) three hard heavy knocks on the hull; (4) the Watcher: a thin high tone like ringing in the ears; (5) possibly the start of a single heavy footstep in the woods.
Clip 2, in order, about every 4 to 7 seconds: (1) one heavy slow footstep in dead leaves far away; (2) a colossal deep groan from under the lake; (3) water gushing in through the boat's seams, a loop; (4) several lantern flames snuffed at once; (5) three muffled thumps from inside a closed metal cooler; (6) a faint sickly glassy shimmer.
