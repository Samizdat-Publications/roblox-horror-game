# Critic consult: strange-boats-r4 (gemini-4-neon)

Date: 11/27/2026. Files shown: 35-boats-before-near.jpg, 36-boats-before-far.jpg, 37-boats-after-near.jpg, 38-boats-after-far.jpg, 39-boats-fisherman-close.jpg. Finish: STOP.

## Question

These four stills are from a co-op Roblox horror fishing game (ages 10 and up, often on phones). On the last night, strange boats appear on the water during the run home. The rule: there are no other boats on this lake, never wave back, never row toward them. Images 1 and 2 are BEFORE (one boat at about 50 studs, then a boat at about 85 studs beside a false beacon on a pole). Images 3 and 4 are AFTER the same two views, and image 5 is a close crop of image 3: the strange boat now lies turned side-on, a tall dark figure in a long coat and a wide brimmed hat stands in it with pinprick eyes, and its lantern hangs from a hooked pole beside his shoulder in a small glow, the boat has raised bow and stern tips, his coat flares and his hat tilts.

Score 0 to 10, BEFORE and AFTER, for each:
1. reads_as_boat: at a glance on a phone, is it clearly a boat with someone in it?
2. distinct_from_beacon: can you tell it apart from the false beacon light on a pole (image 2 and 4)?
3. creepy: does it feel wrong and threatening, not silly?
4. art_fit: does the glow look like part of the world or like a UI sticker?
Then list the three most useful changes, blunt, under 350 words, no em dashes.

## Answer

**Scores (BEFORE / AFTER)**

1. **reads_as_boat: 2 / 7**
Head-on in BEFORE, it reads like a floating crate or buoy. Turning it side-on in AFTER makes it clearly a boat with someone in it on a phone screen, though the flat ends look a bit like a wooden trough.

2. **distinct_from_beacon: 2 / 8**
In BEFORE, both read as a glowing dot on a stick. In AFTER, the wide horizontal hull and human silhouette separate it immediately from the beacon at 85 studs.

3. **creepy: 3 / 5**
AFTER feels genuinely eerie at 85 studs. Up close at 50 studs, the solid black brick torso and wide white eyes look cartoonish, closer to a noir parody than a threat.

4. **art_fit: 6 / 3**
BEFORE blends into the world's lighting. AFTER puts a massive, pale, heavily banded circle behind the figure. It sits behind his chest instead of the lantern bulb and looks like a pasted UI sticker compared to the warm bloom on your own boat's lantern.

**Three Most Useful Changes**

1. **Replace the billboard halo with real lantern light.** Cut the glow radius in half, match the warm amber color of the other lanterns, and center it strictly on the bulb. Drop the banded white disc entirely. Use a subtle real light on the boat planks and water surface to separate the silhouette from the dark horizon.
2. **Fix the eyes and break up the brick torso.** The eyes are too big and too close together, making the face look goofy. Shrink them to faint, slightly separated pinpricks, or remove them completely (a blank shadow under a hat is scarier). Taper the shoulders and flare the coat hem much wider so the body stops reading as a black rectangle.
3. **Slope the bow and stern.** Vertical walls with one tall plank sticking up on the right make the boat look like a wooden trough or bedframe. Angle both ends outward into a clear rowboat taper so the silhouette works on its own.

## Action taken

Round 4 scored 7, 8, 5, 3. Its glow notes describe the earlier large disc, not this image. Taken: the drawn eyes removed (a blank shadow under the hat), the glow set to the lantern amber. Stopped here: reads as a boat went from 2 to 7 and distinct from the beacon from 2 to 8.
