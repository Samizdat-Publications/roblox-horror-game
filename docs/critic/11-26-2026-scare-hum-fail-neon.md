# Critic consult: scare-hum-fail (gemini-4-neon)

Date: 11/26/2026. Files shown: hum_fail.mp4. Finish: STOP.

## Question

Watch and listen to this clip from a co-op Roblox horror fishing game (ages 10 and up, often played on phones). It shows the rule called the Hum, played wrong on purpose.

The rule: the whole frog chorus stops at once, then a deep hum rises from under the boat. The crew must stop reeling, cut the outboard motor, put out the lantern and hold still until the frogs sing again. In this clip the player leaves the motor running, so they should fail: the boat is thrown, the catch goes over the side, the player is pulled under (fade to black, a splash or drag sound), and a paper death card should name the rule ("You left the motor running..." style).

Use this rubric. Score each point 0 to 10, then overall. Quote timestamps for everything. Be blunt, no praise paragraphs, no em dashes, under 600 words.

1. cue_clarity: can you hear the frogs stop and the hum rise? Is it unmistakable, and does it arrive before any danger?
2. learnable: after seeing this once, could a 12 year old say what they should have done?
3. time_to_react: enough time between cue and consequence?
4. payoff: is the fail moment readable and startling rather than confusing? Can you SEE anything happen?
5. relief_or_ending: is there a clear end (death card, fade, sound)?
6. clip_worthy: would five seconds of this make a good short video with no explanation?
7. tone: scary but not disturbing, no gore.
8. broken_or_ugly: anything that undercuts it (black screen, missing sound, glitch, timing).

Then list, in order of impact, the top five concrete fixes, each naming the moment and exactly what to change.

## Answer

**Rubric Scores**

1. **cue_clarity (3/10):** Frogs are audible from 00:00 to 00:03, then cut out during a broken scene reset at 00:04 rather than stopping naturally as a gameplay cue. The hum rises at 00:11, but the intro voiceover talks straight over it until 00:17. Worse, no motor sound plays anywhere in the clip, so players have zero audio warning that the motor is running.
2. **learnable (4/10):** The death card at 00:20 names the mistake ("The motor was running while the frogs were quiet"), and the voiceover hints at it at 00:12 ("If the frogs quit, you quit too"). However, the boat displays oars and says "Row out" at 00:04, with no visible motor or HUD control. A 12 year old will not know what motor was running, how to turn it off, or that lantern and reeling also matter.
3. **time_to_react (6/10):** Roughly seven seconds pass between the hum starting (00:11) and impact (00:18), which is fair for mobile controls. The problem is that the first six seconds are masked by narration (00:04 to 00:17), wasting reaction time.
4. **payoff (2/10):** From 00:07 to 00:17 the screen is nearly pitch black. At 00:17 the camera barely tilts before 00:18 cuts to black with a flat splash graphic and a stock splash sound. You cannot see the boat thrown, no catch goes overboard, and nothing pulls you under.
5. **relief_or_ending (7/10):** The fail state ends clearly. Impact hits at 00:18, a legible paper card appears at 00:20, and the respawn voiceover plays from 00:20 to 00:24. An audio pop at 00:20 slightly Mars the transition.
6. **clip_worthy (1/10):** Nothing happens visually for ten straight seconds (00:07 to 00:17), followed by an unconvincing splash on black at 00:18. No one would watch or share five seconds of this without context.
7. **tone (5/10):** Safe for ages 10 and up with no gore, and the deep hum (00:11 to 00:17) has decent weight. The flat, monotone voiceover (00:04 to 00:17, 00:20 to 00:24) and sudden splash kill the tension.
8. **broken_or_ugly (2/10):** Multiple glaring defects: the clock jumps backward from 10:02 PM (00:03) to 10:00 PM on a hard reset (00:04); the screen is unreadably dark for phones (00:07 to 00:17); the intro VO overlaps the entire Hum event (00:11 to 00:17); oars contradict the "motor" death card (00:04 vs 00:20); and an audio pop fires at 00:20.

**Overall: 3.5/10**

**Top 5 Concrete Fixes (Ordered by Impact)**

1. **Make the scene and capsize visible (00:07 to 00:18):** Raise moonlight rim lighting on the hull, water, and cooler so phone screens can read the boat when the lantern is off. At 00:17, violently roll the boat, launch fish/gear over the gunwale, and plunge the camera beneath the water surface before fading to black at 00:18.
2. **Add motor audio, visuals, and UI (00:04 to 00:17):** Remove the "Row out" prompt (00:04) or swap the oars for an outboard motor. Play a constant rattling engine loop with a visible HUD toggle button whenever the motor is active so players immediately connect the engine noise to the death card at 00:20.
3. **Fix the scene reset and decouple the Hum from intro VO (00:00 to 00:17):** Eliminate the 00:04 reset that rewinds 10:02 PM to 10:00 PM. Keep the frog chorus active until the intro monologue finishes, then drop the frogs to dead silence 1.5 seconds before starting the hum so the cue is unmistakable.
4. **Upgrade the fail sound design and clean the pop (00:18 to 00:20):** Replace the lone stock splash at 00:18 with a heavy hull slam, water rush, and muffled underwater drag sound through 00:19. Trim the harsh audio pop right before "Rough night" at 00:20.
5. **Make the death card teach the full rule (00:20 to 00:25):** Rewrite the card to state the exact recovery steps ("You left the motor running. When the frogs stop, kill the motor, douse the lantern, and hold still.").

## Action taken

All five fixes accepted and given to a shell builder on 11/26/2026: a visible capsize and underwater plunge, a moonlight rim so the boat reads with the lantern out, motor sound and a HUD motor indicator, rule cues duck the tape, layered fail sounds, death cards that state the counter. The clock jump at 00:04 was a test artifact (the scenario reset the night); scenarios now start from a clean reset. Also found in the same session: the drowned view was pure black, and the Neighbor fail clip never framed the Neighbor.
