# Playtest guide

Short for now; S6 writes the full guide. Dates as MM/DD/YYYY.

## Open and play

Build the place and open it in Studio (the main session does this with `tools/studio/launch.ps1`), then press Play. The title screen shows over the lake. The first player in is the host: pick Normal or Nightmare and press Play. Five nights make a run; a failed night is played again while the run has tries left (Normal 3, Nightmare 2). Players who join during a night watch as the drowned until the next one.

The book covers most of the view and stops your hands: no casting, steering, lantern, motor, knocking or bailing while it is open.

## What to try in a run (S4)

1. On the title, read the Lake Rules, pick a difficulty, press Play. Night 1's card shows the quota and the rule taped in tonight.
2. Open the rulebook (R). Tonight's rule has fresh tape. On night 5 one rule is crossed out and rewritten in red: the cue is the same, the answer changed.
3. Clear a night and read the end card: it says what comes next. Fail one: the same night comes back and the card says how many tries are left.
4. Night 5: catch a Steeple Cat by the steeple after 3 AM. At 4 AM the dock bell rings by itself and the lake goes silent: the lantern is locked on and its oil is your clock. Go home without stopping and without going near any other boat or a light that does not blink twice. Home with the fish is dawn and the end of the run.

## What to try on night 1 (S2)

1. Row out of the beacon light (fish do not bite in it) and stop. Cast.
2. When the bobber dips, click at once, then hold and let go to keep the ink mark inside the green band until the strip fills.
3. Look at the fish against the Field Guide card. Keep it (E) or throw it back (X).
4. Row back to the dock end with fish in the hold and press E to bank them. The quota on the paper tag rises.
5. Listen: the frogs should be loud and come from the shore; the radio plays near the dock and fades as you row out. Watch for fireflies along the shore and around the boat.
6. At 5 AM the night ends: clear if the quota is banked, otherwise it repeats.

## Report back

- Anything in the output window that is not the two `[DeadCalm] ... up` lines.
- Whether reeling felt fair (too fast, too slow, the band too narrow).
- Whether a wrong fish was obvious when you looked at it next to the Field Guide.
- Anything too dark to see, or too bright.
