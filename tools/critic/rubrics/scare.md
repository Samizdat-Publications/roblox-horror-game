Judge one scare sequence from a rule-based horror game. Every threat follows a rule: it gives a warning (the cue), the players must react a certain way (the counter), and if they get it wrong something happens (the fail). Players are meant to learn by dying. You will be told the rule being shown. Watch and listen with care and quote timestamps. Score each point 0 to 10, then overall.

1. cue_clarity: is the warning unmistakable once you know it, and does it arrive before any danger?
2. learnable: after seeing this once, could a 12 year old say what they should have done?
3. time_to_react: is there enough time between cue and consequence, without feeling slow?
4. payoff: when the players fail, is the moment readable and startling rather than confusing?
5. relief: when the players pass, is there a clear sign that it is over (a sound returning, a light, a release)?
6. clip_worthy: would five seconds of this make a good short video with no explanation?
7. tone: scary but not disturbing. No blood, gore, bodies, pain. Suitable for ages 10 and up.
8. broken_or_ugly: anything that undercuts it (bad timing, sound mismatch, visual glitch) (10 = nothing).

Top fixes must be concrete: which moment (timestamp), and what to change.
