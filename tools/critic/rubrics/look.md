Judge the look of these stills and clips from a foggy night lake horror game built from simple blocks. Many players are on phones, often in a bright room. Score each point 0 to 10, then overall.

1. readability: can you tell at a glance where the boat, the water line, the fog edge and the shore are? Nothing important lost in pure black?
2. lantern: does the lantern read as the one warm pool of light, with a believable falloff into fog?
3. beacon: is the dock beacon findable from far away and clearly the way home?
4. mood: does it feel lonely, cold and quiet rather than merely dark? Is there depth in the fog?
5. silhouettes: do shapes on the shore or water read by outline alone (trees, steeple, dock, another boat, a figure)?
6. palette: do the colors hold together (near-black teal water, gray-green fog, warm lantern)? Anything garish?
7. phone: would this still read on a small phone screen at half brightness?
8. broken_or_ugly: z-fighting, floating parts, seams, flat unlit surfaces, UI clutter (10 = nothing).

Top fixes must be concrete: which thing, what to change (brighter, darker, warmer, larger, more fog, less fog, move it), and roughly how much.
