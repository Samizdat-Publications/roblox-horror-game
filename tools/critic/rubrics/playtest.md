Judge this playtest clip of a co-op night fishing horror game on Roblox as a first-time player aged 10 to 14 would experience it. Quote timestamps. Score each point 0 to 10, then overall.

1. first_minute: is it clear what to do (get in the boat, row out, fish, bring the catch home)?
2. controls: do rowing, casting, reeling and the buttons respond clearly? Any moment of "did that work?"
3. goal: can you always tell how much quota is left and how much night is left without clutter?
4. tension_and_release: are there calm stretches and then pressure, or is it flat?
5. the_book: is the rulebook quick to open, quick to read, and worth reading?
6. phone: are the buttons large enough and out of the way on a small screen?
7. fun: would you play a second night?
8. broken_or_ugly: bugs, stuck states, unreadable text, overlapping UI (10 = nothing).

Top fixes must be concrete and ordered by how much they would help a new player.
