# Maturity and compliance notes

For Stewart, when he publishes Don't Wave Back and Roblox asks the Maturity and Compliance questionnaire. Roblox changes the wording of these questions from time to time, so match by meaning. Answer honestly for the game as built; if a later update adds anything below marked "No", redo the questionnaire.

## What the game contains

- **Fear:** yes. Dark, foggy setting, tense sound, sudden moments (a lantern going out, a silhouette in the boat, a figure on the shore). No gore. Aim for the lowest option that still says the game has scary or startling content: **Mild** if the choice is frequency and intensity ("occasional, mild fear"), **Moderate** only if Roblox's wording puts jump scares there.
- **Violence:** essentially none. Players are never shown being hurt. A player who breaks a rule fades to black with a splash sound and comes back the next night. Nobody fights anything. Answer **No** to realistic violence, blood and gore. If asked about unrealistic or mild violence, **Mild and infrequent** (a boat is thrown by something unseen; a character is pulled under off screen).
- **Blood and gore:** **No.** None anywhere.
- **Crude humor:** **No.**
- **Romance or sexual content:** **No.**
- **Alcohol, tobacco, drugs:** **No.** The owner mentions coffee.
- **Gambling or paid random items:** **No.** There are no paid items at all. Cosmetics are earned by playing.
- **Strong language:** **No.**
- **Social features:** players communicate with a wheel of fixed phrases only. There is no free-text chat added by the game and no user-generated content. Standard Roblox chat follows the account's own settings.
- **Fishing:** players catch fish and put them in a cooler. No hooks, wounds or cleaning are shown.
- **Real-world references:** none. Fictional lake, fictional owner, no real people or brands.

## Expected result

A content maturity label of **Minimal or Mild** if Roblox reads the fear as mild, otherwise **Mild**. That keeps it open to Liam's account. If the label comes back higher than Mild, tell Claude which answer caused it and the scare in question can be softened.
